The Art of Fragrance

Discover the layers that make our scents unforgettable.

🌸

Top Notes

The first impression, lasting 5–15 minutes. Typically citrus, herbs, or light florals that awaken the senses.

💐

Heart Notes

The soul of the fragrance, emerging after 15 minutes. Often rich florals and spices that form the scent's character.

🌿

Base Notes

The foundation, lasting 6+ hours. Deep musks, woods, and resins that anchor and warm the entire composition.
